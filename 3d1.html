Afterword: What Did You Expect?

You followed every little prompt on the screen. You heated the dinner, you oiled the metal, you painted pretty dots on the barrel, and then you walked into the bedroom and pulled the trigger. You did it because the checklist told you to, and because walking around an empty apartment gets old after two minutes.

That is the entire joke of a comfortable life. There is no big tragedy here, no secret trauma to uncover, and no reason for that gun to be on the table other than to give your hands something to do. When a life is completely padded, safe, and dead from the neck up, you don't blow it apart out of passion.

You do it out of sheer, petty boredom—just to see if the button actually works.
